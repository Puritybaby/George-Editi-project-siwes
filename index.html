Abstract

This technical report documents my six months Industrial Training (SIWES) program undertaken at Channel of Blessing Institute, Goldie Street, Calabar, Cross River State. The program provided practical exposure to the application of Computer Science principles in a real work environment, including software development, hardware maintenance, networking, and ICT support for educational and administrative operations.

Chapter 1: Introduction

1.1 Background of SIWES: The Students Industrial Work Experience Scheme (SIWES) was established by the Industrial Training Fund (ITF) in 1973 to bridge the gap between theory and practice among students of tertiary institutions in Nigeria.

1.2 Objectives of SIWES:

  • Provide avenue for students to acquire industrial skills
  • Expose students to work methods and techniques in handling equipment
  • Prepare students for industrial work situation after graduation
  • Strengthen employers involvement in educational process

1.3 About Host Organization: Channel of Blessing Institute, Goldie Street, Calabar, Cross River State is a faith-based educational and vocational institute committed to youth empowerment, technical training, and community development. The Institute has functional ICT/Media unit that supports its academic and administrative systems.

Chapter 2: Organizational Structure

Organizational Chart: Proprietor/Director, Administrative Department, Academic Department, Technical/ICT Department (Software Unit, Hardware Unit, Networking Unit), SIWES/Interns Unit.

My Departments: I was attached to the Technical/ICT Department and rotated through Software Development, Hardware Maintenance, Networking, and General ICT Support units at Channel of Blessing Institute.

Chapter 3: Work Done & Experience Gained

  • Design and development of responsive websites using HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • Development of simple database applications for student records using Python
  • Computer assembling, troubleshooting, repairs and installation of operating systems
  • Local Area Network (LAN) setup, crimping, router and switch configuration for the Institute
  • Provision of ICT support during training programs, projector setup, and media operations
  • Documentation, data entry, and deployment of project to GitHub Pages: puritybaby.github.io

Skills Acquired: Web Development, Programming, Computer Maintenance, Networking, ICT Support, Problem Solving, Teamwork, Communication, Version Control (Git/GitHub).

Chapter 4: Challenges Encountered

Challenges: Initial difficulty understanding organizational workflow, occasional power outage in Calabar, limited internet bandwidth, and balancing institute activities with technical duties.

Solutions: Guidance from my industry supervisor, self-directed learning via online resources, effective time management, and dedication to duties.

Chapter 5: Conclusion, Recommendation, Acknowledgement

5.1 Conclusion: My SIWES program at Channel of Blessing Institute, Goldie Street, Calabar was a highly rewarding experience. It enabled me to translate theoretical knowledge acquired in the Department of Computer Science, Faculty of Computing, University of Calabar into practical competence and prepared me for future career in computing.

5.2 Recommendation: I recommend that University of Calabar should sustain the SIWES program and continue to approve reputable organizations like Channel of Blessing Institute, Goldie Street, Calabar. The Institute should be supported with more modern ICT equipment to enhance training of SIWES students. ITF should also increase student allowances.

5.3 Acknowledgement: I express profound gratitude to Almighty God, my Head of Department, Department of Computer Science, Faculty of Computing, University of Calabar, my SIWES Coordinator, my Industrial Supervisor at Channel of Blessing Institute, Goldie Street, Calabar, and my beloved family for their support throughout the program.

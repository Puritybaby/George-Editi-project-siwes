Abstract

This report documents my 6 months Industrial Training (SIWES) at Tech Solutions Ltd, Port Harcourt. The training exposed me to practical aspects of Computer Science including web development, networking, hardware maintenance, and customer support, bridging the gap between theory and industry practice.

Chapter 1: Introduction

1.1 SIWES Background: Established by ITF in 1973 to address lack of practical skills among graduates.

1.2 Objectives: Provide practical skills, expose students to work methods, prepare students for labour market, strengthen employer involvement.

1.3 About Host Company: Tech Solutions Ltd, Peter Odili Road, Port Harcourt - specializes in software development, networking, CCTV installation, and IT training.

Chapter 2: Company Structure & My Section

Organization: Managing Director, Admin Dept, Technical Dept (Software, Hardware, Network Units), Interns/SIWES Unit.

I rotated through: Software Development Unit, Hardware Maintenance Unit, Networking Unit, and Customer Support Unit.

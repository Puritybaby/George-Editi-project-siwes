Abstract

This report documents my 6 months Industrial Training at Tech Solutions Ltd, Port Harcourt. Exposed to web development, networking, hardware maintenance and customer support.

Chapter 1: Introduction

SIWES was established by ITF in 1973 to bridge gap between theory and practice. Objectives: acquire industrial skills, expose to work methods, prepare for labour market.

About Company: Tech Solutions Ltd offers software development, networking, CCTV, IT training at Peter Odili Road, PH.

Chapter 2: Company Structure

MD, Admin Dept, Technical Dept (Software, Hardware, Network), Interns Unit. I worked in Software, Hardware, Networking, Customer Support units.

Chapter 3: Activities

  • Websites with HTML/CSS/JS
  • Python apps
  • PC repair & OS installation
  • LAN & Router configuration
  • Client site troubleshooting

Skills: Web Dev, Problem solving, Teamwork, GitHub Pages

Chapter 4: Challenges

Initial difficulty with large codebase, power failure, transport. Solved via tutorials, supervisor guidance, early resumption.

Chapter 5: Conclusion & Recommendation

Experience rewarding. Applied classroom knowledge. Recommend: extend SIWES to 12 months for CSC, provide transport stipend, structured intern training.
